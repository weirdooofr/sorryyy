Laila & Majnu

A letter of truth, written for

Maddam jii

In a world of fleeting moments, you are my eternal Laila…

and I am the foolish Majnu who forgot that love is a promise, not a performance.

Touch the seal, Maddam jii

My Dearest Maddam jii,

I am writing this without a single defence, because I have none to offer.

The Instagram post I made to make you jealous, and the screenshots from that fake account, were foolish and immature. I wish I could call it a moment of madness, but I chose it, one step at a time. It came from my own insecurity and a desperate hunger for your attention. Instead of telling you honestly that I felt afraid or unseen, I played a game with your feelings. That was cowardice dressed up as cleverness.

There are no excuses here. Playing with someone's feelings is wrong, and it is worse when that someone is the person I love most. You were hurt, and you had every right to be. Blocking me was not an overreaction. It was you protecting your peace from someone who had not earned your trust. I don't ask you to undo it. I understand it, and I respect it.

❦

Here is what I need you to know. You are the only person who holds my heart. No one else was ever in the picture, and nothing I did was a sign that you were not enough. It was a sign that I was not brave enough to ask for what I needed.

So this is my promise, and I mean to keep it in actions rather than words. No fake profiles. No posts meant to provoke. No games, ever. If I feel insecure, I will tell you plainly, to your face. I pledge complete transparency and real respect for your feelings and for your boundaries, including the one you have just drawn.

If you need more time, take it. I will wait, not as a demand but as devotion. And if you decide to let me try again, I will spend every ordinary day showing you that broken trust can be rebuilt, one honest day at a time.

Forever yours, in every version of our story,

Your Majnu

Can you find it in your heart?

Waiting for your heart…

Your Majnu
